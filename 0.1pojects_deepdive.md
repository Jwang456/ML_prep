# Sql Bridge project

## STAR structure

### Situation:

- Internal business partner who handles database-related request
- Manually, 20 people 50% of time, estimated annual labor cost of over $900k
- automate the SQL query generation process and reduce dependency on manual data retrieval.

### Task:

- Lead from PoC
- to building a scalable, production-ready system.

### Action

- deep-dive sessions with business partners to understand their data workflows and typical query intents
- PoC phase to validate that LLM-based query generation
- Explore how industry does this. 
- Schema preprocessing, to parse and abstract database metadata into structured prompt-ready representations;
- Domain-specific prompt tuning
  - OpenAI embedding API to vectorize table name, desc, business jargons etc...
- validation: execution and human intervention

### Results

- automated the majority of routine database query requests, reducing manual workload for the support team by over 80%. labor cost savings of approximately $800,000.
- execution accuracy above 80%, with a human intervention rate maintained below 10% in production.

## 5 deep dives

### Why: What motivated this project? Why not just use manual data teams?
The main driver was the overwhelming volume and complexity of internal data requests, especially for legal and business reporting. Manual processing was slow, error-prone, and expensive—at Ford, a 20-person team spent over 50% of their time on routine SQL queries, costing nearly $900,000 annually. Automating this process with an LLM-based NL2SQL solution not only reduces costs and turnaround time, but also democratizes data access for non-technical stakeholders.

### HOw: How did you architect and implement the solution? Why did you choose LLM APIs over rule-based or fine-tuned models?

- Schema metadata is pre-cached for speed
- User queries are processed via API backend, which dynamically constructs prompts using relevant schema and few-shot examples selected via embedding similarity
- SQL generation and post-validation are fully automated

We chose API-based LLMs for their rapid adaptability to evolving schemas and language patterns, as well as cost-effectiveness compared to maintaining a fine-tuned in-house model.


# Tradein

## STAR

### Situation

- part of Ford’s broader customer experience improvement plan
- provide customers with a transparent, instant, and guaranteed trade-in price when they visited a dealership
- Traditional tradein can be annoying, resulting in inconsistent valuations and eroding customer trust.
- empower both customers and dealers with an objective, data-driven valuation tool.

### Task

- Lead the end-to-end development of the automated trade-in appraisal platform
- Mainly on the machine learning pipeline
- data sourcing and quality assessment, through model development, training, and hyperparameter optimization, to deployment and ongoing monitoring of the model in production on Google Cloud Platform

### Action

- data selection process, evaluating both dealer-collected trade-in records and 2M+ auction records across 50 states,  We ultimately prioritized auction data due to its greater volume and standardization
  - MSRP-based trim standardization method to improve feature reliability
- data processing pipeline that included robust feature engineering and standardization routines
- For modeling, we developed two XGBoost models: one incorporating color and region features, and another excluding them
- As an innovation, I led the development of an internal ML package called VVS-ML (Vehicle Value Solution)
  - confident index, monotone constraint, stratified sampling, bayesian optimization + grid search in VvsML

### Results

- As a result of this initiative, we achieved an estimated $3.5 million annual cost savings—derived from reduced manual labor and avoided third-party contractor expenses.
- Our model consistently outperformed industry standards such as KBB and Carvana in valuation accuracy, giving both customers and Ford a stronger basis for transparent trade-in offers
- However, we did encounter some resistance from dealerships. While our system increases transparency for customers and aligns with Ford’s brand values, some dealers were hesitant to fully promote the platform—since it can reduce their ability to negotiate and capture additional margin from trade-ins. As a result, daily usage volume has not yet reached its full potential, which limits the immediate business impact.
- Looking forward, we are planning to launch the guaranteed price feature directly within the FordPass app.
